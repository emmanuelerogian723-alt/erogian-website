Cybersecurity: The Most Recession-Proof Career in Tech

Cybersecurity: The Most Recession-Proof Career in Tech

In a world of data breaches and cyber attacks, security professionals are the immune system of the internet.

Why Cybersecurity Is Always Hiring

Every company with a website, app, or database is a target. The threats are growing:

  • Ransomware attacks increased 200% in 2025
  • The average cost of a data breach is $4.45 million
  • There are 3.5 million unfilled cybersecurity jobs globally

What You'll Earn

  • Information Security Analyst: $110,824/year
  • Penetration Tester: $112,597/year
  • Security Architect: $164,532/year
  • Incident Response Manager: $120,711/year

How to Get Started (Free)

  • Learn cybersecurity fundamentals on SkillForge
  • Understand networks, encryption, and threats
  • Practice with ethical hacking tools
  • Build a home lab and practice
  • Get certified (CompTIA Security+, then CEH)

You don't need a degree. You need skills and a portfolio of security projects.
