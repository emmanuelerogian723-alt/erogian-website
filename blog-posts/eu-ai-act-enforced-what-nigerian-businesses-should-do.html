The EU AI Act Is Now Being Enforced — What Nigerian Businesses Should Do Before Using AI at Scale

AI regulation is no longer a future conversation. The European Union has entered a new enforcement phase for its AI Act.

What changed?

The EU AI Act classifies AI systems by risk. Companies that show sensible AI processes will look safer to customers, partners, and procurement teams.

Why should a Nigerian business care?

  • International clients may ask how your chatbot or lead system handles personal information
  • Your business may use AI tools hosted abroad with their own data terms
  • Customer trust depends on transparency about AI use

Use AI deliberately, document how it is used, and keep a human accountable for important decisions.
